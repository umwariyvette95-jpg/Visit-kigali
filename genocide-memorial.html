Remembering — So It Never Happens Again

The Kigali Genocide Memorial is the final resting place of more than 250,000 victims of the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi. It is both a site of burial and a powerful museum dedicated to education, remembrance, and the prevention of genocide worldwide.

"This is not a place of sadness alone — it is a testament to the resilience of the Rwandan people and their extraordinary journey toward reconciliation."
