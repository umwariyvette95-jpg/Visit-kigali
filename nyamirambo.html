The Heartbeat of Old Kigali

Nyamirambo is Kigali's oldest and most colourful neighbourhood — a place where the city truly comes alive. Mosques, churches, bustling markets, and artistic walls coexist in perfect rhythm.

"Nyamirambo is where Kigali keeps its heart. Walk its streets and you walk through decades of history, culture, and resilience."

🚶 Walking Tour Tips

  • Start at the Women's Center for context
  • Wear comfortable, respectful clothing
  • Try the mandazi (fried dough) from street vendors
  • Ask locals before photographing people

🌙 Nightlife Highlights

  1. Catch live music at Chez Lapin
  2. Sample brochettes from roadside grills
  3. Join locals for a Primus or Mutzig beer
  4. Explore the lit-up mosque at night
